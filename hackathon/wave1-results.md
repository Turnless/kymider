## What the judges' comments rewarded

- "Your contract compiled cleanly … defines N circuits" and "genuine private
  state with a witnesses.ts and a commitment/hash".
- "Your tests exercise the contract through a simulator and assert real state."
- "A react frontend is present. It sets up the full wallet provider stack. The
  UI calls the contract and reads on-chain state back."
- "A deployed contract address is documented."
- A real-world problem with a clear buyer, and domain expertise or live use.
- What they asked for next: say exactly which parts need privacy, add audio to
  the video, and make clear who benefits.

## Crowding

About 22 entries pitched credit, solvency or "prove you qualify", including
WhisperScore ("Prove you qualify, without exposing your life"), Vantage, datum,
ZeroScore, HORIZON, proofFi, Nocturne and Phrim. The sponsor's own brief lists
"prove solvency" as its finance example. The finance entry that scored targeted
one named fraud (double-pledged invoices), not generic creditworthiness.
Hence the Wave 2 pitch: one concrete number, 110% collateral instead of 150%.
